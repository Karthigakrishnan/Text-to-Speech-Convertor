Text-to-Speech Converter Project
Overview
Welcome to the Text-to-Speech Converter! 🚀 This project allows you to turn your text into speech with just a few simple commands. Whether you need to hear your favorite quotes, listen to messages while doing something else, or simply enjoy the magic of speech synthesis, this is the tool for you! 🎤

Key Features:
Choose your voice: Select between a male or female voice to suit your style. 👨‍💻👩‍💻

Adjust speech speed: Make it slow and soothing or fast and energetic, all based on your mood! 🏃‍♂️💨

Real-time interaction: Type in any text and hear it spoken right away. No need for pre-programmed scripts—it's all about you.

Repeat or exit: After hearing the speech, you can decide to repeat the process or exit gracefully.
